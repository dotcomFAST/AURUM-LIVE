01 · Interactive prototype

Play a full round.

This is not a video — it’s the real flow. The timer, bets, payouts and history all work, so you can feel every state the way a player would.

How to play

  1. Join the table from the lobby — the featured card has one clear action.
  2. Pick a chip and tap any number, dozen, column or outside bet before the timer ends.
  3. Undo, Clear, Double or Rebet — the four actions players use most, always in thumb reach.
  4. Watch “No more bets” lock the board, then see the result, payout breakdown and win animation.
  5. Open History from the lobby tab bar — every round you played is there.
The first round always lands on 17 Black, so you can see the win state right away. After that, results are random.
02 · Key states

One table, three moments.

A live round lasts about 40 seconds. The interface has to tell the player, at a glance, what they can do right now.

Betting open

Countdown ring sits on top of the stream, where the eyes already are. Placed chips show their value right on the cell.

No more bets

The timer turns red and the controls fade to 35% — nothing can be changed, and the UI says it without a pop-up.

Win

Result first, amount second, breakdown third. The lost Red bet fades, the winning cell glows on the board.

03 · Design decisions

Why it looks like this.

01

Stream on top, controls at the bottom

The video takes the upper 44% of the screen; everything you tap lives in the lower half.

Players hold the phone with one hand. Chips, actions and the board are all inside the natural thumb zone.
02

The full board, not a simplified one

All 37 numbers, columns, dozens and outside bets fit in 358 px without scrolling or switching tabs.

Regular players expect the classic layout. Hiding numbers behind a tab costs time in a 15-second window.
03

Timer over the stream

A ring countdown in a glass pill, centred above the results strip. It turns red in the last 5 seconds.

The timer is the most important information on the screen, so it sits where attention already is — on the dealer.
04

States without pop-ups

“No more bets” doesn’t interrupt. It recolours the timer and dims what can’t be used.

Modal dialogs over a live stream block the moment players are waiting for — the ball dropping.
05

Win = number, amount, breakdown

The winning number, then the amount in gold, then a short line explaining which bets paid.

Clear payout logic builds trust. Players should never wonder why they won €885 and not €900.
06

Built for regulated markets

A session timer is always visible on the table, 18+ is marked, and losses in History stay neutral — no alarming red.

Responsible-gaming signals are a requirement in regulated markets, so they’re part of the layout from day one.

05 · Motion

The win moment, timed.

Principles

Motion never blocks betting: the result card disappears on its own before the next round opens. Durations stay under one second, ease-out for entrances. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion.

The particle burst and count-up are prototyped in code; the same timing is ready to hand off as Lottie / After Effects.
